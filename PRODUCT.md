# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents and caregivers of infants (newborn–24 months), often sleep-deprived and
low on patience. Primary context: **on a phone, in a dark room, one hand free**,
during or around a night feed — but also planning ahead at a laptop or on the
couch. Many are anxious about whether they're "doing it right." Some are parenting
a premature baby and need gestational-age adjustment. They want a fast answer, not
a reading assignment.

## Product Purpose

Turn a baby's birthday, wake time, wake-window lengths, and bedtime into a clear
daily nap schedule — with a 24-hour visual breakdown and a check against published,
cited sleep guidance for the baby's age. State lives in the URL so a plan is
shareable with a partner or caregiver. Success = a tired parent gets a trustworthy,
readable plan in seconds and feels reassured rather than judged.

## Brand Personality

**Calm, trustworthy, clear.** Reassuring clinical calm — evidence-forward but never
cold. Quiet confidence: it knows the science and shows its sources, but speaks to a
worried parent gently. Voice is plain and direct; no jargon, no hype, no baby-talk.
The tier badges and cited sources carry the trust; the tone carries the calm.

## Anti-references

- **Generic baby-app pastel** (the named anti-reference): no cutesy pastel-pink/blue,
  no rounded-blob nursery stickers, no cartoon aesthetic. Warmth comes from tone and
  restraint, not decoration.
- **Cold medical dashboard**: no EHR-density gray-on-gray tables, no alarm-red.
- **Anxious / alarmist framing**: out-of-range values inform, they don't scold. A
  parent who's already worried must not be made to feel they're failing.
- **Generic SaaS scaffold**: no hero-metric template, no tracked-uppercase eyebrows
  on every section, no identical card grids, no gradient text.

## Design Principles

- **Reassure, don't grade.** Guidance frames ranges as normal variation, not pass/fail.
  Colour and copy inform without alarming.
- **Show your work.** Every recommendation is tied to a cited, credentialed source and a
  Tier 1/2 badge. Trust is earned by transparency, not asserted.
- **Answer first, detail on demand.** The schedule and visual breakdown are the headline;
  evidence, sources, and explainers expand only when the parent asks for them.
- **Built for tired thumbs.** One-hand phone use in a dark room is the default case:
  generous tap targets, low glare, forgiving inputs, minimal cognitive load.
- **Quiet by default.** Restraint is the aesthetic — calm surfaces, one accent, typography
  and spacing doing the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 (harder on the dark slate surface — verify, don't
assume). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus states. `prefers-reduced-motion`
honored on every animation. Tier badges encode meaning by shape + text, not colour alone
(color-blind safe). Decorative imagery hidden from screen readers (already done). Large,
thumb-reachable tap targets for the one-hand-in-the-dark case.
